Cognitive Talent Engine (CTE)
The Architecture for Identifying True Competence
CTE is an open-source framework for identifying and verifying high-potential talent through Cognitive Assessment.
We are moving past the legacy paradigm of "Credit Scores" and "Resume-based filtering"—metrics that rely on the past and are easily manipulated by social signaling. Instead, we are building a system that verifies Intent and Execution in the present.
Core Philosophy
Legacy systems evaluate humans based on their history. CTE evaluates humans based on their present cognitive state and demonstrable capability.
Cognitive Intent (Ethics Score): We utilize Agentic AI to strip away masks. We detect manipulation, exploitation, and philosophical evasion (the "noise") to ensure that resources are directed only toward those with authentic intent.
Proof of Competence (Action Score): We move beyond resumes. Our framework employs real-time verification to measure whether a participant can actually deliver on a value proposition.
Why CTE?
For Builders: CTE provides a direct path to resource allocation based on your ability to deliver, not who you know or what you have done in the past.
For Architects: We are building a protocol that allows autonomous systems to distinguish between value creators and value extractors.
No More Noise: We eliminate middlemen, social manipulators, and data harvesters who currently dominate the recruitment and talent-matching landscape.
Repository Structure
/core: Logic for Intent-based Cognitive Assessment.
/patterns: Configurable templates for detecting behavioral patterns (Manipulators, Harvesters, Evaders).
/docs: Technical documentation on our "Proof of Action" framework.
